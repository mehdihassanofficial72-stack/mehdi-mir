Who We Are

Education With Purpose

The Wisdom School & College is an educational institution located near Alamdar Road, Back Market, Danyore, Gilgit.

Established in 2022, the institution provides education from Class 5th to 12th and aims to provide students with a strong academic foundation in a disciplined and supportive environment.

We believe education should develop not only academic knowledge but also confidence, character, responsibility, creativity and practical skills.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide high-quality education in a disciplined and supportive environment while developing students' knowledge, character, confidence and practical skills.

Our Vision

To prepare students to become knowledgeable, confident, responsible and capable individuals who can contribute positively to society and their future.
